# Educational creator Reel direction

This editable format is available as **Ali Abdaal-inspired** in Reel Builder and Auto edit. Its API identifier is `creator-education`.

## Reference and design decisions

Reference inspected: [Ali Abdaal, 5 Productivity Apps I Use Every Day](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/DDzQTEiEOWk). Two playback frames showed a central speaker, supporting imagery above the speaker, and short dark captions on a light rounded panel. The frames are visual references, not a measurement of the entire video's pacing or audio mix.

The Cutline treatment uses original, editable educational graphics: a cream supporting area, dark readable type, restrained accent colors, numbered steps and a clear takeaway. The user's footage and voice remain the source material. This preset does not add the reference creator's likeness, branding, claims, footage or voice.

## Composition and motion

- Reserve the upper region for one supporting idea at a time. Cards and B-roll should not occupy the same time and space.
- Keep the speaker clear in the lower region. Framing is fixed and editable; it does not track a face automatically.
- Keep captions in short source-aligned groups, below the speaker's face and within the safe area.
- Use a small sentence-aligned punch-in for emphasis, with a readable hold and a restrained return.
- Vary callouts, numbered steps and takeaway cards without adding decorative underlines, unnecessary effects or invented statistics.
- Keep source footage, captions, B-roll, motion layers and audio independently editable.

## Review contract

Check entrances and settled holds, the longest card and caption, clear separation of supporting visuals, continuous source audio, append and replacement behavior, and one-step Undo/Redo. Exercise both builders using real local transcription. Export and fully decode a vertical H.264/AAC test Reel before installing the release.
