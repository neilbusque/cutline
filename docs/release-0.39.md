# Cutline 0.39

The effects short-form video runs on today, native and editable.

- Eleven new transitions: flash, blur dissolve, zoom in, zoom out, whip left and right, push up and down, spin, shake and glitch, each at its usual length by default
- Punch out, pop in and shake camera moves
- Letterbox, flash cut, glitch and halation finishing overlays
- Impact (Hormozi), Bounce and Neon caption looks, with Arial Black and Impact as font choices
- Speed ramps: one clip cut into contiguous pieces at different speeds, with four presets and an assistant action
- Progress bar, notification banner and subscribe button recipes
- Six sound accents made on this Mac: whoosh, impact, pop, click, riser and ding, placed two frames early at half volume
- The Effects panel shows transitions beside looks, camera moves and overlays

See [Trending effects](trending-effects.md). Earlier: [0.38](release-0.38.md).
