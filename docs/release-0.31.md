# Changes in 0.31.0

## Creator-style Shorts

Turn a speaker video into a Short in one of three styles measured from real creators: **Split explainer** (Jack Roberts-inspired), **Card canvas** (sebintel-inspired) and **Locked split** (Matthew Berman-inspired). In **Assistant**, choose the matching suggestion and send. The assistant plans the beats from your transcript, puts the real logos and pages you mention on screen as you name them, cuts the speaker between split, full-screen and punch-in framing, adds the style's backdrop and motion, and restyles the captions. Everything stays editable and undoable. See [Creator-style Shorts](creator-styles.md).

Assistants use `cutline_style_reel` directly.

## Captions

- Dark caption text on the light card canvas no longer has a dark halo.
- Re-splitting captions never leaves two captions on screen in the same frame.

## Logos and pages

- One-color logos are recolored only when they would vanish against the background (GitHub on dark).
- Wikipedia fundraising and site notices are removed from page screenshots.
