# Direction and variety

The recurring failure is a headline above a small card, the same pop-in, a new accent color, then another card. More animation does not change the underlying composition or reveal new information.

Before styling, write the promise and its visible payoff. A software film should show an input, an operation and the resulting state. A process story should show work developing into a finished artifact. A personal performance can remain with one subject if their delivery and gestures continue the story. A chart needs real values, a map needs a real route, and a claim needs evidence.

## Choose a form

| Form                  | Primary subject                            | Useful progression                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Presenter tutorial    | Real speaker and exact supporting evidence | Assertion, proof split, legible full-screen detail, consequence   |
| Faceless product film | Real interface or product                  | Context, action, changed state, useful result                     |
| Diagram explanation   | A persistent relationship                  | Establish nodes, connect, transfer, receiving state, whole system |
| Data explanation      | A verified dataset                         | Baseline, comparison or change, labels, conclusion                |
| Process story         | Coherent real work                         | Situation, attempt, detail, work in progress, outcome             |
| Kinetic statement     | One short idea                             | Impact, development, complete readable phrase, resolution         |

Pick from the material and story. Missing presenter footage is a format constraint. A static avatar or generic typing clip does not substitute for a person explaining a real operation.

## Plan beats with a fingerprint

Record purpose, dominant subject, composition, scale, action/state change, source passage, transition and sound. Adjacent fingerprints may match when an action continues. Review four or more similar title/card beats especially closely: does anything develop beyond the text? Change the mechanism or material when the answer is no. Do not randomly rotate layouts to satisfy a quota.

Examples of meaningful variation include a split giving way to its proof, a wide interface moving to a legible control, a packet arriving at a receiver, a comparison resolving, a path reaching its destination, or a quiet close-up allowing a thought to land. A backdrop changing pink to blue has no such meaning by itself.

## Connect scenes

Choose a shared object, edge, word, pose, visual center, direction or color field. Define the outgoing and incoming poses. Sample immediately before and after the cut and during any overlap. Native matching transforms are editable 2D, not arbitrary morphing or a real 3D camera.

Use a hard cut when it gives the explanation clarity. A cut is a valid motion choice. Use longer holds for complex screens and actual actions, shorter emphasis for a simple turn, and a settled reading pose for essential type. No fixed shot interval, maximum word rule or tempo fits every format.

## Close the actual story

Show the promised outcome and finish the spoken thought. A loop may connect an ending object back to its opening state, but only when requested or useful and when sound joins cleanly. Avoid the same montage CTA, logo sting or generic slogan on every film.
