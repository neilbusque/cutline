# Color and finishing

Correction, shot matching and artistic treatment are separate decisions. A single filter at the same strength does not make different sources match.

1. Choose an anchor shot for the sequence. Compare adjacent frames at ordinary display brightness.
2. Correct brightness, contrast and saturation per shot. Compare neutral whites, dark detail and skin. Preserve source information instead of making every dark source darker.
3. Inspect consecutive cuts. Match the perceived subject and neutral surfaces, with allowances for a deliberate change in location or scene.
4. Choose the creative look and strength for this sequence. Protect UI, logos, diagrams and meaningful colors on separate neutral clips.
5. Inspect a face, the lightest screen, deepest shadow and busiest caption frame, then watch the cuts in motion. Recheck after export.

## Starting treatments

| Look         | Starting strength | Use                                                                        |
| ------------ | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `none`       | Neutral           | Faithful product screens, logos and already-correct material               |
| `studio`     | 0.70              | Gentle contrast/saturation lift for clean presenter footage                |
| `soft-film`  | 0.60              | Softer contrast and restrained warmth for an editorial sequence            |
| `crisp-mono` | 0.85              | A deliberate reduced-color comparison or insert; use 1 for full monochrome |

These are starting values chosen by Cutline, not measured settings from any creator's film. Existing cinematic, warm, cool, vintage, vivid and other looks remain available. Stronger treatments need a reason and comparison against the source.

**Copy look to this track** copies only the look and its strength. Each clip keeps its individual brightness, contrast, saturation, blur and vignette. This is useful when a presenter sequence has already been corrected shot by shot.

**Apply this look to all video & photos** also copies the selected brightness, contrast, saturation, blur and vignette. Use it only when that is intended. Neither button analyzes a target shot or performs automatic color matching.

Current controls are global CSS filters shared by preview and export. Cutline does not offer calibrated scopes, white-balance temperature/tint controls, selective skin masks, LUT import, HDR color management or true film emulation. Do not label a preset as a verified LUT or claim selective correction that was not performed. Use suitable original media or an externally corrected source when the job requires those tools.

Finishing texture follows the subject. A personal recollection may benefit from a little grain; a software control generally benefits from clean pixels. Grain, crushed blacks, a vignette and black bars cannot repair weak direction.
