## Recommended Cutline translation (authored recommendations)

- Define an effect's purpose before selecting it: reveal structure, demonstrate direction, compare states, guide attention, imply depth, or mark a narrative change.
- Build native editable mechanisms for chart growth, path drawing/map reveal, perspective screen tilt, foreground/background separation, orbital relationship diagrams, timed countdown, and connected object transitions. Use verified data for charts and maps; never fabricate business proof.
- Keep effect intensity and camera motion as scene decisions with readable holds. Use one dominant action at a time and avoid making all layers bounce.
- Add a color workflow that distinguishes source correction, shot matching, and creative look. Grade skin and neutral UI separately when masks/layers permit; never crush small UI text into a fashionable dark grade.
- Offer at least three direction modes: presenter-supported tutorial, faceless product demonstration, process story. Decide from footage and story; do not silently turn missing presenter footage into another card montage.
- Require a proof/payout shot: the chart changes, route completes, input produces output, state comparison resolves, or finished scene is shown. A title announcing the outcome is not the outcome.
- For sound, record spot points and intended roles, then inspect in playback. These visual-only samples support no claim about sound balance, exact hit timing, or which SFX library was used.
