# Cutline 0.34

Motion direction now has a dated reference library for Jack Roberts, Nick Automates and Aasil Khan, with an inventory of 867 public videos and detailed observations from selected passages. It separates observed techniques from chosen treatments, with practical direction for distinct visual actions, connected scenes, effects, color, captions and sound. The guide is available to built-in and connected assistants through `motionReferences`, the director guide and effects guide.

Creator-style beats can choose their entrance, backdrop, background, emphasis, framing and proof-video source time. A clean cut or fade can replace the repeated pop/ripple treatment, and proof passages can continue instead of restarting. Editorial review notes identify repeated compositions and source passages without forcing a random effect rotation. Older omitted options preserve their treatment.

Aasil Khan is the primary film-quality reference. The assistant has an **Aasil-inspired motion film** starting prompt, with distinct depth-demo, process-film and kinetic-world directions. Editable plane tilt/perspective and Creator studio scenes support layered depth and larger connected visual compositions. These are 2.5D planes, not a true 3D renderer.

**Motion > Scenes > Editorial** includes three original editable scenes: Transfer, Compare and Focus. Each develops a visual relationship or state change, with named layers, portrait/landscape layout and duration retiming. They are illustrative 2D mechanisms, not fabricated product evidence or 3D reconstruction.

**Effects** adds Studio, Soft film and Crisp mono, with adjustable starting strength. **Copy look to this track** preserves each shot's individual corrections. Reset also clears RGB split and inversion. These remain shared native CSS looks rather than LUTs or automatic shot matching.

**Stock > Openverse** searches photos and audio without an API key. Preview a sound, download it into the library or place it on a separate audible Sound effects track. Source and license credits remain attached to the local asset. Search excludes noncommercial/no-derivatives licenses, while attribution and share-alike obligations may still apply. Current supported hosts are Flickr/Wikimedia images and Freesound audio. Openverse does not supply video B-roll.
